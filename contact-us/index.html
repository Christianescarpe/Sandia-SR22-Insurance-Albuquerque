Preparation

What Information to Have Ready

To get you an accurate quote and prepare your filing quickly, please have the following details ready when you reach out:

  • Your New Mexico driver's license number (or state ID number)
  • Any notification letter received from the New Mexico MVD or local court
  • Vehicle information (year, make, model, VIN), or note if you need a non-owner policy
  • Details regarding the citation or event that prompted the filing requirement

If you do not have all these documents right now, do not worry — call us anyway and we can begin pulling your information together. To understand how the certificate works before calling, review our New Mexico SR-22 essentials page.

What Happens Next

We review your situation, explain available policy tiers, and discuss whether a traditional policy or non-owner liability solutions fit your budget best. Once you choose coverage, we submit your SR-22 certificate electronically to the New Mexico Motor Vehicle Division.
