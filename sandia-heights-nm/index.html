What Prompts an SR-22 Requirement

Common triggers include:

  • A DWI offense or administrative per se suspension
  • Driving an uninsured motor vehicle
  • Accumulating excessive moving violation points
  • License suspensions resulting from missed court appearances
  • Complying with ignition interlock requirements
